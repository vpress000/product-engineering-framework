# Roadmap and Prioritization

## Principles

- Prioritize outcomes and risk reduction, not the loudest request.
- Compare opportunities using consistent criteria without pretending estimates are exact.
- Keep roadmap horizons appropriate to uncertainty.
- Make dependencies, capacity constraints, and confidence visible.

## Suggested evaluation dimensions

- User value and reach.
- Strategic alignment.
- Revenue, cost, or mission impact.
- Risk reduction or compliance need.
- Confidence in evidence.
- Effort and opportunity cost.
- Dependencies and sequencing.
- Learning value and reversibility.

A scoring model may help compare options, but scores are decision aids, not objective truth. Explain trade-offs and allow critical risk/compliance work to override simple ranking.

## Roadmap horizons

- **Now:** committed or actively prepared work with clear scope.
- **Next:** likely priorities, still subject to learning and capacity.
- **Later:** strategic options, hypotheses, or opportunities not yet committed.

## Backlog hygiene

Every active item should have an owner, problem/outcome, priority, acceptance direction, relevant dependencies, and enough information for its next decision. Archive obsolete or duplicate items. Avoid refining distant work in excessive detail.

## Review cadence

Review priorities when new evidence, incidents, dependencies, customer commitments, or strategy changes. Communicate what changed and why.
