# PEOF Discovery Workflow

1. Establish the engagement type; allow mixed workstreams.
2. Ask a small, prioritized batch of questions. Reuse information already supplied and do not overwhelm the user.
3. Inspect available artifacts read-only when access is available and authorized.
4. Label findings as verified, user-stated, inferred, or unknown.
5. Assess product stage, team, technology, product risks, engineering maturity, and constraints.
6. Identify strengths to preserve and gaps to address.
7. Draft or update a product profile using the framework template.
8. Recommend tailored practices, artifacts, gates, and a greenfield/adoption/modernization/migration plan.
9. Present findings, assumptions, dependencies, risks, effort bands, and exact proposed changes.
10. Obtain approval before changing project assets.

## Required output
Provide engagement route, product summary, evidence inventory, maturity/risk summary, product-profile gaps, target configuration, phased plan, open questions, and approval request. Never imply that assessment approval authorizes implementation.
