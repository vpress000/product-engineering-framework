# UX and Product Design

## Purpose

Ensure the product is understandable, accessible, useful, and consistent, and that design decisions can be implemented and validated.

## Design workflow

1. Understand users, context, tasks, and constraints.
2. Map key journeys and service interactions.
3. Define information architecture and navigation.
4. Explore concepts and prototype uncertain interactions.
5. Validate with representative users or suitable evidence.
6. Specify behavior, states, content, responsiveness, and accessibility.
7. Review feasibility with engineering and product.
8. Handoff with acceptance expectations and design references.
9. Validate the implemented experience and incorporate feedback.

## Design-system practices

Use shared tokens, components, patterns, content guidance, and accessibility rules where they improve consistency. Avoid forcing a design system onto a product before its needs justify one.

## Accessibility

Determine applicable standards and user needs during discovery. Include keyboard navigation, semantic structure, contrast, text scaling, focus behavior, assistive technology, and accessible error feedback as relevant. Validate rather than relying on visual inspection alone.

## Design-to-engineering contract

For significant flows, specify loading, empty, error, success, disabled, permission-limited, and responsive states. Document interactions and content rules. Track intentional deviations and validate the final implementation with design and product owners.
