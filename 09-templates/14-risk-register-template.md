## Scoring guidance
Define likelihood and impact scales for the product before scoring. Consider user harm, business impact, legal/contractual exposure, service interruption, data loss, recovery complexity, and reputational impact. Do not treat a numeric score as a substitute for expert judgment.

## Escalation and review
Record who may accept each risk, any compensating controls, expiry/review dates, and triggers for reassessment. Silence is not acceptance.
