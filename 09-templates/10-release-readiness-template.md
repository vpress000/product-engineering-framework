# Release Readiness Checklist — [Release]

- **Release owner:** [name/role]
- **Version/build:** [identifier]
- **Planned window:** [date/time/time zone]

## Scope and approvals
- [ ] Release scope and exclusions are documented.
- [ ] Product acceptance is recorded.
- [ ] Required technical, security, privacy, and compliance approvals are recorded.

## Quality evidence
- [ ] Required automated checks passed.
- [ ] Acceptance and regression testing completed.
- [ ] No unresolved defects above approved risk tolerance.
- [ ] Known issues and limitations are documented.

## Operational readiness
- [ ] Monitoring, dashboards, and alerts are ready.
- [ ] Support owner and escalation path are known.
- [ ] Runbooks and release notes are updated.
- [ ] Capacity, dependency, and access configuration are checked.

## Deployment and recovery
- [ ] Deployment steps are reviewed.
- [ ] Data migrations and backups are validated.
- [ ] Rollback or forward-recovery strategy is understood and feasible.
- [ ] Feature flags/cohort controls have owners and expiry/review dates.

## Post-release
- [ ] Smoke tests and critical journeys are defined.
- [ ] Observation window and success signals are defined.
- [ ] Go/no-go and halt authority are explicit.

## Decision
[Go / no-go / conditional go], decision owner, rationale, conditions, timestamp.
