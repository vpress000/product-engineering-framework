# Product and Engineering Assessment

## Assessment approach

Assess capabilities using evidence, not impressions alone. Review documents and system artifacts where authorized, interview relevant people, and compare intended process with actual practice.

## Maturity scale

| Level | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unknown | No reliable evidence or assessment not performed |
| 1 | Ad hoc | Practice is inconsistent, person-dependent, or reactive |
| 2 | Repeatable | Basic practice exists and is used in common cases |
| 3 | Defined | Practice is documented, understood, and consistently applied |
| 4 | Measured | Outcomes and process performance are measured and acted upon |
| 5 | Adaptive | Practice is continuously improved using evidence and feedback |

Do not target level 5 everywhere. Choose maturity appropriate to risk and value.

## Capability domains

Score relevant domains from 0–5 and provide evidence, confidence, risk, and next action:

1. Product strategy and outcomes.
2. Discovery and user research.
3. Requirements and scope management.
4. UX and design system.
5. Architecture and technical decision-making.
6. Planning and dependency management.
7. Engineering practices and code health.
8. Testing and quality assurance.
9. Security, privacy, and compliance.
10. CI/CD and release management.
11. Production operations and reliability.
12. Documentation and knowledge continuity.
13. Governance, ownership, and continuous improvement.
14. AI-assisted engineering controls, when applicable.

## Risk assessment

For each material gap, record:

- Risk statement: condition, event, and consequence.
- Likelihood and impact (use a product-appropriate scale).
- Affected users, services, data, or business outcomes.
- Existing controls and evidence.
- Mitigation and owner.
- Residual risk and acceptance authority.

## Findings classification

Classify each finding as:

- Strength to preserve.
- Critical risk requiring action.
- High-value improvement.
- Efficiency opportunity.
- Documentation/traceability gap.
- Technical debt.
- Unknown requiring further investigation.

## Assessment output

Produce an executive summary, evidence inventory, capability heatmap, risk register, list of strengths to preserve, prioritized recommendations, and confidence/limitations statement. Do not assign a maturity score without explaining its evidence.
