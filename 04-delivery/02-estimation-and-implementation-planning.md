# Estimation and Implementation Planning

## Planning objectives

Create a credible sequence of work, reveal uncertainty early, and enable delivery of valuable increments. Estimates are forecasts with uncertainty, not promises of exact duration.

## Plan contents

For each implementation group, define:

- Desired outcome and scope boundaries.
- Requirements and acceptance conditions.
- Technical design and key decisions.
- Dependencies, predecessor work, and external constraints.
- Work breakdown and ownership.
- Complexity and uncertainty (separate these where possible).
- Risks, assumptions, and open questions.
- Test and review strategy.
- Integration, deployment, and rollback considerations.
- Completion evidence and release target, if known.

## Estimation

Use a method suitable for the team: relative sizing, effort bands, historical throughput, or ranges. State confidence and assumptions. Use technical spikes for high-uncertainty items rather than false precision.

## Sequencing

Sequence by value, risk, dependency, learning, and capacity. Establish integration checkpoints. Identify work that can proceed in parallel without creating unstable shared interfaces or excessive coordination.

## Planning cadence

Plan near-term work in detail and distant work at a higher level. Re-plan when evidence changes. Keep uncommitted options separate from committed scope.

## Completion

A group is complete when the agreed scope is implemented, reviewed, tested, accepted, documented to the agreed level, and operationally ready for its intended release or explicitly marked as not yet releasable.
