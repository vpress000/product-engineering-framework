# Risk and Decision Management

## Risk register fields

- ID and date.
- Risk statement (condition, event, consequence).
- Category: product, delivery, architecture, security, privacy, compliance, operations, supplier, or people.
- Likelihood and impact, with scale definitions.
- Affected users, assets, and objectives.
- Existing controls and evidence.
- Treatment: avoid, mitigate, transfer/share, or accept.
- Action owner and due/review date.
- Residual risk and authorized acceptance owner.
- Status and links to decisions/incidents.

## Decision record fields

- Decision ID, title, date, status, and owner.
- Context and problem.
- Constraints and requirements.
- Options considered.
- Decision and rationale.
- Consequences and trade-offs.
- Risks and mitigations.
- Revisit trigger/date.
- Related decisions and artifacts.

## Escalation

Escalate decisions that exceed the owner's authority, materially affect security/privacy, violate legal or contractual obligations, create irreversible data loss risk, change product commitments, or increase production risk beyond approved tolerance.

## Accepted risks

Acceptance must be explicit, time-bounded where practical, and approved by the person authorized to own the impact. Record compensating controls and review triggers. Silence is not acceptance.
